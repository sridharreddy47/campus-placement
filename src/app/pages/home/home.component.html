About Campus Placement Cell

Bridging Academia & Industry Excellence

Our Campus Placement Management System (CPMS) automates student registration, job opportunity publication, merit-based CGPA eligibility screening, and interview tracking. Designed to adhere strictly to institutional fair-play policies.

  • ✓ 100% Verified student academic data with CGPA thresholds
  • ✓ Real-time status updates: Applied, Shortlisted, Selected, Rejected
  • ✓ Single-click application with instant eligibility validation
